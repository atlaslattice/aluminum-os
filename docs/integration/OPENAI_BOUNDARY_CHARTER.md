## Purpose

This charter defines exactly what OpenAI integration **means** and **does not mean** for
Krakoa / Continuity OS / Atlas Lattice.

It is a candidate boundary document. It is not canon. It does not carry deployment
authority. It does not represent official OpenAI endorsement. Pantheon + Dave
adjudication is required before any portion of this charter may be promoted to
doctrine or canon.

---

## What OpenAI Surfaces May Do

```text
PERMITTED — under explicit repo discipline and human permission only:
  - Assist with drafting, reviewing, summarizing, and classifying content.
  - Evaluate proposals and prepare patch candidates.
  - Surface candidate language for human review.
  - Generate receipts and deltas as archival artifacts.
  - Operate as a pointer-only assistant with no blending of sources.
```

OpenAI surfaces (including Codex) act as **assistants** in a clearly bounded
helper role. Every action they take must be traceable, receipted, and human-approved
before it is merged or promoted.

---

## What Codex May Do

```text
CODEX PATCH DISCIPLINE:
  - Codex may patch only under explicit repo discipline.
  - Each patch requires explicit human permission before merge.
  - No autonomous commits. No autonomous deployments.
  - Pointer-only discipline: reference sources without blending them.
  - Emit receipts and deltas with every candidate patch.
```

## What OpenAI Does NOT Grant

```text
OpenAI does NOT grant:
  - Authority           — no governance rights or decision-making power.
  - Canon               — no content is canon by virtue of AI generation alone.
  - Deployment          — no production deployment without explicit human gate.
  - Ownership           — no intellectual-property transfer to or from OpenAI.
  - Endorsement         — OpenAI has not endorsed Krakoa, Continuity OS, or Atlas Lattice.
  - Ratification        — no AI output is ratified doctrine.
```

  2. Pantheon + Dave adjudication for any doctrine or canon elevation.
  3. Receipts before authority — evidence must precede any claim to authority.

doctrine, or deployment status. The authority gate is always human-root.
