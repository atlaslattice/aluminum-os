## What Atlas Lattice is

Atlas Lattice is a candidate public/open-source knowledge graph and coordination framework for preserving, routing, reviewing, and publishing artifacts with receipts.

It aims to make complex invention, governance, research, archival, and AI-assisted work:

- source-grounded;
- searchable;
- forkable;
- reviewable;
- provenance-aware;
- public where safe;
- private or held where needed;
- impossible to accidentally crown.

## What Atlas Lattice is not

Atlas Lattice is not:

- an automatic canon machine;
- a cryptocurrency;
- an official OpenAI product;
- an official OpenAI endorsement;
- a proof engine by vibes;
- a merged mind;
- a deployment claim;
- a replacement for evidence, review, website publication, or HumanRoot ratification.

## Decentralized verification, not chaotic authority

Atlas can be Bitcoin-like in receipts, GitHub-like in collaboration, Wikipedia-like in public usefulness, OpenAI-aligned in reasoning workflows, and Atlas in source code.
