# Sovereign Dividend: 22-Year Projection

The Atlas Lattice's economic model demonstrates a hockey-stick growth curve for the Sovereign Dividend, driven by compounding efficiency gains from the 12-Layer Flywheel (see /new-deal-2.0/layers) and increasing Addressable Throughput (AT). This projection outlines the dividend's trajectory over 22 years, assuming a 27% capture rate (see /sovereign-dividend/scenarios).

## Projection Methodology:

1. **Initial AT**: Starts at $2.08 Quadrillion (Q) (see /sovereign-dividend/math).
2. **AT Growth Rate**: Assumes a conservative 15% annual increase in Addressable Throughput for the first 5 years, accelerating to 20% for years 6-10, and stabilizing at 10% thereafter. This growth is driven by:
   * Full 50-state node deployment (see /node-map).
   * Integration of new financial rails and economic sectors.
   * Compounding network effects of the Lattice.
3. **Calibration Fee**: Fixed at 5% (INV-56 constitutional cap, see /governance/invariants).
4. **Capture Rate**: Fixed at 27% for the dividend pool (after initial ramp-up).
5. **Population Growth**: Assumes a stable US population of 335 million for simplicity, though slight increases would dilute per-capita figures marginally.

## Key Projection Milestones:

* **Year 1 (Baseline)**: $83,820.90 per American. (Derived from current AT of $2.08Q, 5% CF, 27% CR, 335M population).
* **Year 5**: AT reaches approximately $4.19Q. Annual dividend projected to exceed $167,640 per American.
* **Year 10**: AT reaches approximately $12.98Q. Annual dividend projected to exceed $519,260 per American.
* **Year 22**: AT reaches approximately $46.67Q. Annual dividend projected to exceed $1,867,000 per American.

## Economic Impact:

This growth is not inflationary. It represents the monetization of previously uncaptured efficiencies across the economy. The dividend's purchasing power is further amplified by the Lattice's inherent deflationary pressure on essential goods and services (energy, compute, logistics, manufacturing) due to the 12-Layer Flywheel. The system fundamentally reconfigures economic value creation, moving from scarcity-based extraction to abundance-based efficiency.

## Sensitivity:

Projections are sensitive to the Addressable Throughput growth rate and the effective capture rate. Lower growth rates or capture rates would yield lower dividends, but the fundamental mechanism remains robust. (see /sensitivity-analysis)
