# Sovereign Dividend: Derivation Math

The Sovereign Dividend is earned revenue, not a handout. It is the direct return to citizens from the efficiency gains generated by the Atlas Lattice's constitutional infrastructure operating system. The calculation is transparent and auditable.

## Core Formula:

$$ \text{Annual Sovereign Dividend} = \frac{(\text{Addressable Throughput} \times \text{Calibration Fee} \times \text{Capture Rate})}{\text{Eligible Population}} $$

## Variables:

1. **Addressable Throughput (AT)**: The total economic value transacted across financial rails that the Atlas Lattice directly optimizes and routes.
   * Fedwire: $1.13 Quadrillion (Q) annually (2023 data).
   * CHIPS: $450 Trillion (T) annually (2023 data).
   * Commercial Transactions: $500-700 Trillion (T) annually (estimate for ACH, card networks, and other digital payments directly benefiting from Lattice-enabled efficiency).
   * Current Baseline AT: $1.13Q + $450T + $500T = $2.08 Quadrillion/year. (This figure is dynamic and grows with Lattice integration and economic expansion.)

2. **Calibration Fee (CF)**: The constitutional cap on the fee applied to Lattice-routed transactions.
   * INV-56: Establishes a maximum 5% fee on gross transaction value (see /governance/invariants). This fee is structurally embedded, not a tax.
   * Current Rate: 5%.

3. **Capture Rate (CR)**: The percentage of the Calibration Fee allocated to the Sovereign Dividend pool.
   * INV-56 Split: The 5% Calibration Fee is constitutionally split:
     * 40% to Sovereign Dividend (CR = 0.40)
     * 25% to Node Operations
     * 20% to Infrastructure Reserve
     * 15% to Governance & Research
   * Canonical CR for Dividend Calculation: 27%. This is the effective rate after accounting for initial operational ramp-up and reserve allocations. (see /sovereign-dividend/scenarios)

4. **Eligible Population (EP)**: All citizens within the jurisdiction receiving the dividend.
   * US Baseline: Approximately 335 million (2024 estimate).

## Canonical Calculation (US Baseline, 27% Capture Rate):

Given:
* AT = $2.08Q
* CF = 0.05 (5%)
* CR = 0.27 (27% of the 5% fee)
* EP = 335,000,000

Dividend Pool = $2.08Q × 0.05 × 0.27
Dividend Pool = $2,080,000,000,000,000 × 0.05 × 0.27
Dividend Pool = $104,000,000,000,000 × 0.27
Dividend Pool = $28,080,000,000,000$ (i.e., $28.08 Trillion)

Annual Sovereign Dividend per American = $\frac{\$28,080,000,000,000}{335,000,000}$

Annual Sovereign Dividend per American = $83,820.90
