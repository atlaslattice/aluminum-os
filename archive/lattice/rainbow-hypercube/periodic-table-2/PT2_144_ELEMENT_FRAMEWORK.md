## Isotopes and Variants

Every element in the 144 table has a family of isotopes and excited states:
- Ground state + metastable states
- Isotopic variants with different neutron counts
- Charged states and excitonic complexes
- Topological variants (different defect configurations or anyonic statistics)

Each isotope/variant inherits the parent element’s 12D position but carries a small offset vector and additional property deltas (especially in coherence time, resonance frequencies, and optical/acoustic signatures).
