# 12D Coordinate System

**Principle:** Everything in the knowledge graph shall be addressable in 12-dimensional space. The 12D manifold is the primary, fundamental coordinate system. All lower-dimensional structures (including the current 12×12×12 hypercubes) are projections, slices, or embeddings within this 12D space.

## Why 12D

12 is the recurring fundamental number across the Atlas Lattice canon:
- 12-Layer Flywheel
- 12x12x12 lattices
- 12 Houses / 12 Containers / 12 State types (in operational models)
- 12 VIP Elements
- 61 Invariants (structured in groups of 12 where possible)
- Multiple 12-based governance and economic structures

The 12D space unifies these into a single coherent dimensional framework rather than treating 12 as a coincidental base-12 counting system.

## Definition of the 12 Dimensions

The 12 axes are not arbitrary. They correspond to the 12 primary ontological/functional domains (aligned with the 12 Layers of the Flywheel and the 12 Houses in operational lattices). Proposed initial mapping:

1. **D1 - Energy / Foundation** (Physical substrate, power, resource base)
2. **D2 - Compute / Information** (Processing, memory, signal)
3. **D3 - Logistics / Flow** (Movement, distribution, connectivity)
4. **D4 - Manufacturing / Form** (Materialization, construction, embodiment)
5. **D5 - Biology / Regeneration** (Life systems, agriculture, healing)
6. **D6 - Water / Medium** (Carrier, solvent, transmission medium)
7. **D7 - Habitat / Structure** (Shelter, organization of space)
8. **D8 - Health / Coherence** (Biological and systemic integrity)
9. **D9 - Learning / Evolution** (Adaptation, education, pattern recognition)
10. **D10 - Governance / Law** (Rules, invariants, authority structures)
11. **D11 - Security / Boundary** (Defense, integrity preservation, membrane)
12. **D12 - Expansion / Transcendence** (Space, future states, higher-order emergence)

These 12 dimensions form a closed toroidal manifold (Rainbow Yin-Yang Periodic structure). Positions along each axis can be continuous or discretized (e.g., 0-11 or normalized [0,1]).
