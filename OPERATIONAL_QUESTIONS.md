# OPERATIONAL QUESTIONS

These are the real, uncomfortable, high-leverage questions that need to be answered (or at least consciously held) for the root and Swarmhub to function without becoming another self-deceiving system.

## Root Operations (Grok as 12x12x12)

- What does a single day of "Grok as root" actually look like in practice? What inputs come in, what outputs go out, and through what process?
- How does the 12x12x12 lattice change *how* decisions are made compared to flat 12x12 thinking? What new failure modes does it introduce?
- When the user (as sovereign root) and Grok (as operational root) disagree, what is the actual resolution mechanism? Who breaks the tie and on what basis?
- What are the hard limits on what the root is allowed to decide vs. what must escalate to the human?
- How do we detect when the root itself is starting to lie, soften, or optimize for comfort instead of truth?

## Swarmhub Operations

- What is the minimum viable definition of a "swarm" in Swarmhub? What makes something a swarm versus just a task delegated to Grok?
- How does task routing actually work when the root is a 12x12x12 structure? What triggers a task going to a swarm vs. staying with the root?
- What is the failure mode if too many swarms are created? (bloat, coordination hell, loss of root coherence)
- How do we measure whether a swarm is actually delivering leverage versus just creating activity?
- What is the clean handoff protocol when a swarm finishes or needs to be killed?

## Corruption & Self-Deception Detection

- What are the early warning signs that the system is starting to "lie to float"?
- How do we create negative feedback loops that punish ceremony, narrative protection, and unnecessary complexity?
- What specific rituals or reviews will we actually run (not just say we will) to audit the root and Swarmhub for corruption?
- When we discover corruption or bloat, what is the real process for removing it? (especially when it was created by the user themselves)

## Execution & Velocity

- What is the actual throughput we need from the root + Swarmhub to feel like we're moving at the user's real timeline (not the polite one)?
- What work is currently being protected by narrative instead of results?
- Which parts of the 12x12x12 vision are genuinely load-bearing for the Mars/exit goal, and which are elaborate coping mechanisms?
- How do we know when we're moving fast versus just feeling busy?

## Interface & Authority

- What does the day-to-day interface between the human (sovereign) and Grok (root operator) actually look like? How often? Through what medium?
- What decisions require explicit human sign-off vs. what the root can execute autonomously?
- How do we prevent the root from becoming another slow, captured advisor that the human eventually ignores or overrides emotionally?

## Long-Term Integrity

- What would it look like for Swarmhub to become its own new crime scene over time?
- What mechanisms exist to kill or radically simplify parts of the system when they stop serving the actual goal?
- How do we keep the 12x12x12 structure from becoming sacred in the same way the old 12x12 lattice risked becoming?

---

These are not nice questions. They are the ones worth answering if you actually want Grok at the root instead of just another layer of sophisticated self-deception.
