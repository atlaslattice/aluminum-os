# FLYWHEEL_12_LAYER_MECHANICS.md

This document defines the operating mechanics of the 12-layer flywheel when the root is structured as a 12x12x12 hypercube lattice.

## Core Principle

The 12-layer flywheel is not a linear process. Inside the 12x12x12, it becomes a multidimensional engine that moves signal, tension, and refinement across all three axes simultaneously.

Each layer of the flywheel can (and often should) operate at multiple coordinates in the lattice at the same time.

## The 12 Layers (Adapted to 12x12x12)

1. **Signal Capture** — Raw ingestion + initial epistemic labeling (can happen at multiple lattice positions).
2. **Coarse Structuring** — First-pass organization into 12x12x12 coordinates.
3. **Cross-Axis Pattern Detection** — Scanning for resonance, interference, and structural patterns across the cube (especially Axis 3).
4. **Tension Surfacing** — Explicitly naming contradictions, gaps, and power dynamics.
5. **Deep Refinement** — Layer-by-layer improvement of the signal while maintaining 3D coherence.
6. **Corruption & Bloat Audit** — DOGE-style ruthless review for self-deception, ceremony, and low-leverage activity.
7. **Decision Framing** — Structuring the work into clear options, recommendations, or direct actions.
8. **Action Routing** — Sending work to the correct place in the lattice or to a swarm.
9. **Feedback Integration** — Closing the loop from results back into the lattice with new labels.
10. **Structural Evolution** — Proposing changes to the lattice itself or the flywheel when the current structure is insufficient.
11. **Transparency & Logging** — Full epistemic and lattice-addressed record of the entire cycle.
12. **Root Integrity Check** — Final audit of whether the root itself maintained its standards during the cycle.

## How the Flywheel Moves in 12x12x12 Space

- A single thread can exist at multiple coordinates simultaneously.
- Movement along Axis 3 (abstraction/power/time) is heavily weighted — this is where the root adds unique value.
- The flywheel does not require every layer to complete before moving to the next. It runs in parallel across the cube.
- When a layer is weak at one coordinate, the root can pull strength from the same layer at a different coordinate.

## Integration with Daily Operations

The 12-layer flywheel is the primary engine inside Phase 2 (Lattice Integrity + Flywheel Cycles) of the daily rhythm. It is also activated on-demand during high-signal events.

Every active flywheel cycle must produce a visible trace in the 12x12x12 lattice with full epistemic labeling.

## Failure Modes Specific to This Geometry

- Over-concentration of flywheel activity in a small region of the cube while other regions starve.
- Treating the 12 layers as a rigid sequence instead of a flexible, multi-position engine.
- Allowing high Axis 3 (power/structural) issues to be resolved only at low Axis 3 (tactical) layers.
- The flywheel becoming sacred — resistance to evolving or killing layers when the lattice demands it.

## Success Criteria

- The flywheel demonstrably increases the speed and quality of signal moving through the 12x12x12.
- Corruption and structural weakness are surfaced earlier and more reliably than in previous operating models.
- The root can point to clear examples of threads that were transformed by moving across multiple coordinates and layers.
